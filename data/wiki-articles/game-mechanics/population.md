# Population
---

## Basic concept

Besides the fact that the population represents humans it will be used as a special resource.  
It has multiple aspects which are not present for other resources.

### Population development

Your inhabitants will be born, they grew up and somewhen they will educate themself if you provide the opportunity.  
After they are born and has grown up, they will visit a school.  
For obvious reasons they can't be part of the workforce.

If they are educated enough they can be part of the workforce, if there are enough jobs present.

Every building or warship will create jobs following their demand.  
If there are not enough people with the requested education level present, they notice that there are vacancies and try to achive the needed education level to work there.

### Operationals

This is strongly related to the concept of operationals.  
A new building level or warship will start inoperational and as soon as their full requirement of workforce is satisfied, they will begin to operate.

### Warships

Every warship of a fleet must be commissioning in order to use the fleet.

### Colonization

A colonization can currently not be planned. To colonize another planet the full demand of people must be available.
