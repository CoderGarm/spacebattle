# Transport and Migration
---

## Transport

For every planet it can be set what is allowed to be delivered and what is needed.  
If a delivery could really be done depends on the availability of the demanded resources or military personnel.

The transport companies of your empire will execute the jobs, searching the best routes and deliver at the time.

---
For obvious reasons you can command your military, but not your civilians.  
They will detect opportunities on other planets on their own and will move to planets with better working conditions.
