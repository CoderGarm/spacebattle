## War Harvest

The upcoming release will introduce the wormholes, lets celebrate it with an event of epic propotions.

I would say, everyone should have the chance to conquer a planet.  
The rules are simple:

##### Rules - tbd

- Every fleet with the name "Conqueror" will be placed in the home system of the owner at the first tick of the event.
- In a time frame of two weeks, every fleet with that name can conquer a planet by entering the orbit of an inactive player's planet first and alive.  
  The conquered planet need to be indicated by a forum post in the corresponding thread.

It would be nice if there is a little fluff in it. Strong emotions, flying missiles and
losing the reactor's the containment are accepted favorably.  
It would be cool if the snippets of the forum posts would be used to make some noise at reddit or if it would be allowed that we can post it there.

##### Conditions - tbd

1. The timeframe will be something like 01.01.'24 to 14:01.'24.
2. The inactive players will be listed at the beginning. The planets and resources will be upgraded in beforehand. The fleets will be restored to the initial three 'Songbird'
   destroyer with around 85 k tons.
   If an inactive player returns, the planet will be removed from the FFA-List and the fleet will be upgraded (message me ingame) in order to take part in the event.
3. Every interested player without an hyper-space capable fleet can send me a PM with an amount of warships by class up to 170 k tons - twice the initial fleet.
4. Some NPCs will be added for the timeframe. They have bigger fleets and a bigger gain.

##### What is the win?

1. An entry in the event ladder with the destroyed and lost ship tonnage and conquered planets for the war harvest event!
2. New planets with a lot of shipyard capacity!
