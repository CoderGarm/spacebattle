# Pirate hunt
---

### Goal

The **pirate hunt** will staff police forces in order to secure the space around the mission areas.  
The forces sent will patrol the area and protect trade routes.

#### Deployment of naval forces

Warships can be detached from fleets or a full fleet or flotilla can be taken to fulfil police missions.  
The operation area must be defined and the forces will reduce the trade jamming activities in the area.
