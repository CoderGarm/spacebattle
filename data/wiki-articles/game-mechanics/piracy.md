# Piracy
---

### A dangerous galaxy

There are countless reasons for illegal activities and the space inhabited by humans is not necessarily safe.  
In spacefare, pirates are a common threat and nearly everywhere.

Countless **officially uninhabited places** are hideouts for any kind of groups outside the civilized society.

---

#### Occurrence

Nearly every place will be visited by pirates as long as there are reasons for piracy.  
The question unfortunately isn't how to redeem piracy, it it how to avoid and survive it.

To protect your commercial and private shipping, it is advisable to set up police forces.
